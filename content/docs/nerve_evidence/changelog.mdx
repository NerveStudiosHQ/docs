## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15) [#v1-0-0]

#### Added
- Evidence collection at crime scenes: shell casings, blood, fingerprints and trace DNA on vehicles and doors, seized firearms and general physical evidence.
- Sealed evidence bags with serial numbers, tamper recording and a permanent chain of custody.
- Forensic laboratories with a submission bench, processing queue and analyst skill check.
- Fingerprint, DNA and ballistic identification, with sample ageing and contamination.
- Investigations with investigators, evidence, scenes, suspects, vehicles, notes, lab reports and a timeline.
- The forensic workstation with every section, and exports for other resources.
