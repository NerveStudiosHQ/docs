## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16) [#v1-0-0]

#### Added
- Evidence collection at crime scenes: shell casings, blood, fingerprints and trace DNA on vehicles and doors, seized firearms and general physical evidence.
- Sealed evidence bags with serial numbers, tamper recording and a permanent chain of custody.
- Forensic laboratories with a submission bench, processing queue and analyst skill check.
- Fingerprint, DNA and ballistic identification, with sample ageing and contamination.
- Investigations with investigators, evidence, scenes, suspects, vehicles, notes, lab reports and a timeline.
- The forensic workstation with every section, and exports for other resources.
- Search the whole workstation from one box: press Ctrl+K or `/` to find evidence codes, bag serials, plates, case numbers, people and firearm serials.
- An overview that shows what needs you: new lab results on your cases, tampered bags, unsealed samples losing quality and ground evidence about to wash away.
- Scene timers showing how long uncollected evidence has before it washes away.
- Open cases, link evidence to them and add suspects from anywhere in the workstation.
- A lab queue with each sample's place in line and estimated ready time, and withdrawing a queued sample.
- A case report and forensic reports you can copy as plain text.
- Sorting on the evidence and investigation lists, and keyboard navigation (Escape steps back one layer at a time).
