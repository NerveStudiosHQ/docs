## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20) [#v1-0-0]

#### Added

- Priority queue: players get in by points, then by who came first.
- A live queue card with the player's place, wait, estimated wait, priority and status, plus a queue list and a personal priority page.
- Priority from the database, Discord roles, temporary grants, reconnect grace and your own system through `shared/editable.lua`.
- Players who crash or disconnect while waiting get their place back if they return within the grace window.
- Discord through the Nerve Studios bot (nothing to host) or your own bot, with priority by role, names, avatars, a members-only option and a role whitelist.
- Reserved slots and a bypass list for staff.
- A startup hold, spacing between joins and a cap on players loading at once, so a full queue never hitches the server.
- txAdmin restart awareness: joining pauses right before a scheduled restart.
- Queue length in the server list name.
- Manual approval mode for your own resources, with a timeout and fallback.
- `queue` admin command: status, list, info, kick, grant, revoke, add, delete, open and reload.
- Exports and events for other resources.
- English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch and Portuguese.
