Nerve Studios Queue holds players on the connecting screen when your server is full and lets them in by priority. While they wait, they see a live card with their place in line, how long they have waited, an estimated wait and their priority, plus a queue list and a page that shows where their priority comes from.

It runs on the server only, works on ESX, QBox or any other framework, and needs nothing but ox_lib and oxmysql.

## Features

- **Priority queue.** Players get in by points, and players with the same points get in first come, first served.
- **Live queue card** with the player's place, wait time, estimated wait, priority and a status line, plus a queue list and a personal priority page.
- **Priority from everywhere:** saved priority in the database, Discord roles, temporary grants, reconnect grace and your own donator or store system.
- **Crash protection.** A player who crashes while waiting gets their place back when they return within the grace window. A player who crashes while playing gets reconnect priority.
- **Discord** through the Nerve Studios bot or your own bot: priority by role, Discord names and avatars on the card, a members-only option and a role whitelist.
- **Reserved slots and a bypass list** for staff.
- **No join hitching.** A startup hold, spacing between joins and a cap on players loading at once.
- **txAdmin aware.** Joining pauses right before a scheduled restart, and starts again if the restart is skipped.
- **Queue length in the server list** in front of your server name, if you want it.
- **Manual approval** for your own resources, with a timeout and fallback.
- **Admin command** to see the queue, kick, grant temporary priority and save priority.
- **Exports and events** for other resources.
- **Six languages:** English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch and Portuguese.

## Performance

- Server only. Players download nothing from this resource.
- The queue loop runs twice a second and only does work for players who are waiting.
- Discord lookups are cached, so players reconnecting over and over never hammer Discord.
- The console stays silent unless you turn on `log` or `debug`, apart from warnings about config mistakes you need to fix.
