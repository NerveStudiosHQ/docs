Nerve Studios Watermark shows your server's logo in a corner of every player's screen. It sways or bounces gently, fades out while the pause menu is open, and stays put on ultrawide monitors. Each player can pick a logo, move it, resize it and change how it moves from a settings panel, within the limits you set. Photo Mode hides it for clean screenshots and clips.

It runs on ESX, QBox or a standalone server with only ox_lib installed. The settings panel ships ready to use, with no build step.

## Features

- **Any number of logos.** Use png, jpg, jpeg, webp, gif or svg files from the resource's `images/` folder, or an `https://` link.
- **A default logo** for new players, and logos you only offer to some players, such as a job mark or a VIP mark.
- **Animations:** sway, bounce, or both together, with configurable speed, strength, motion curve and fade in and out.
- **Placement presets** for top left, top centre and top right.
- **Position editor.** Players drag the watermark anywhere, resize it from the corners, nudge it with the arrow keys, and snap it to a grid and alignment guides.
- **Player settings panel** to pick a logo, position, size, opacity and animation, with a live preview, **Save**, **Cancel** and **Reset all**.
- **Photo Mode** hides the watermark and brings it back on its own after a set time, or when the player turns it off.
- **Hides in the pause menu.** The watermark fades out while the pause menu or map is open.
- **Ultrawide safe.** On 21:9 and wider monitors the watermark stays inside a centred 16:9 area.
- **You decide what players may change**, with limits for size, opacity and where the watermark may be dragged.
- **One option to reset everyone's saved settings** after a big change.
- **Your accent colour** on the panel and editor, and your own logo in its side menu.
- **Exports** so other resources can hide the watermark, toggle Photo Mode or open the settings.
- **Translatable.** Every player-facing text is in the locale file.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [ox_lib](https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib/releases) | The only dependency |
| Framework | None needed. Works on ESX, QBox or a standalone server |

There is no database and no SQL to run.

## Performance

- The watermark runs on each player's game. The server only checks once per start whether a newer version is out.
- About 0.00 ms in the client resmon while idle.
- The console stays silent unless `debug = true`, apart from warnings about config mistakes you need to fix.
