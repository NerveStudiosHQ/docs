## Commands

| Command | Alias | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/watermark` | `/wm` | Opens the watermark settings panel |
| `/togglewatermark` | `/togwm` | Turns Photo Mode on or off |

Every player can use both. Change the names under `commands` in `shared/config.lua`. The first name in each list is the command and the rest are aliases. Add more aliases, or set a command to `false` to turn it off:

```lua
commands = {
    settings  = { 'watermark', 'wm', 'logo' },
    photoMode = false,
},
```

The Photo Mode command is also not registered when `player.photoMode` is `false`.

## Settings panel [#settings-panel]

`/watermark` opens a panel with five sections:

| Section | What the player can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Pick one of the logos you offer |
| **Placement** | Pick top left, top centre or top right, drag the logo on a small preview of the screen, or open the position editor |
| **Motion** | Turn the animation on or off and pick a style |
| **Look** | Show or hide the watermark, and change its size and opacity |
| **Photo Mode** | Start or stop Photo Mode |

Changes preview live on the player's screen. **Save** keeps them, **Cancel** or Escape closes the panel and throws them away, and **Reset all** puts everything back to your defaults. Anything you locked under `player` in `shared/config.lua` shows as **Set by server**.

Settings are saved on the player's own computer for your server, so they stay after reconnecting and server restarts. To reset every player at once, see [Resetting everyone's settings](/nerve_watermark/configuration#resetting-everyones-settings).

## Position editor [#position-editor]

Opened from the **Placement** section with **Fine-tune on screen**:

- Drag the watermark to move it, and drag a corner to resize it.
- Arrow keys nudge it. Hold Shift to move it further.
- It snaps to a grid and to guides at the screen centre and edges. Players can switch snapping off in the editor.
- Enter applies the new position and Escape cancels it.

Players can only place the watermark inside the area set by `limits.bounds`. With `player.changePosition = false`, they cannot move it at all.

## Photo Mode [#photo-mode]

Photo Mode hides the watermark for screenshots and clips. It starts from `/togglewatermark` or from the **Photo Mode** section of the panel, and closes the panel when it starts.

- With `photoMode.duration` above `0` and `photoMode.autoRestore = true`, the watermark comes back on its own after that many seconds (60 by default).
- With `duration = 0` or `autoRestore = false`, it stays hidden until the player runs the command again or stops it in the panel.

Photo Mode is not saved. It ends when the player leaves the server. Turn it off for everyone with `player.photoMode = false`.
