---
title: "Exports"
description: "Client exports other resources can call to open, close and check the Nerve Studios Animation Positioning tools."
---

Other resources can open and close each tool on the player's own game, for example from a radial menu or a phone app. All exports are **client side**: call them from a client script.

| Export | Returns | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `OpenAnimPos()` | nothing | Opens animation positioning |
| `CloseAnimPos()` | nothing | Closes positioning and ends any placed position |
| `IsAnimPosOpen()` | `boolean` | Whether the positioning editor is open |
| `OpenPhotoCam()` | nothing | Opens the photo camera |
| `ClosePhotoCam()` | nothing | Closes the photo camera |
| `IsPhotoCamOpen()` | `boolean` | Whether the photo camera is open |
| `OpenFreeCam()` | nothing | Opens the free camera |
| `CloseFreeCam()` | nothing | Closes the free camera |
| `IsFreeCamOpen()` | `boolean` | Whether the free camera is open |

The open exports run the same checks as the keys: the tool must be enabled, the player needs its permission, no other tool may be open, and the fight and weapon blocks apply. When a check fails, the player gets the usual message. See [When a tool won't open](/nerve_animpos/controls#when-a-tool-wont-open).

## Example [#example]

```lua
-- Open the photo camera from your own menu
exports.nerve_animpos:OpenPhotoCam()

-- Close whatever is open before a cutscene
if exports.nerve_animpos:IsPhotoCamOpen() then
    exports.nerve_animpos:ClosePhotoCam()
end
if exports.nerve_animpos:IsFreeCamOpen() then
    exports.nerve_animpos:CloseFreeCam()
end
exports.nerve_animpos:CloseAnimPos()
```
