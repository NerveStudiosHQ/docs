## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19) [#v1-0-0]

#### Added
- Checking, savings, shared, business, society and government accounts, each with its own account number that players can copy from the dashboard and the account page.
- Deposits, withdrawals and transfers at bank branches, with limits and fees per transaction and per day, and a confirmation step that shows who receives the money.
- Transfers to one of your own accounts or to a player standing nearby in one click, or to any account number.
- A ledger behind every account, so balances can always be checked against their history.
- A banking screen laid out like a bank app: total balance, account cards, recent transactions, your debit card, weekly spending and today's limits. The side menu rests as a row of icons and opens with labels on hover; show your server's logo at its foot with `ui.logo` in `shared/config.lua`.
- A lock screen when the banking screen opens: players unlock it with their debit card PIN, or hold a button if they have no card. Set `ui.unlock` to 'pin', 'hold' or false and `ui.unlockMinutes` for how long it stays open.
- Players' Discord profile pictures in the bank (`ui.avatars` in `shared/config.lua`).
- ATMs that show the bank on the machine's own screen, with the camera facing it and the mouse working on it, plus a full-screen ATM terminal for machines that can't. Admins line a model's screen up in game with `/atmcalibrate`, saved for every player.
- A bright ATM screen with side-key options, a keypad and take-your-cash and take-your-card steps.
- Every ATM on the base map is listed in `shared/config_atms.lua`, and map ATMs only open at those spots. Add the ATMs your map mods place there, or set `trustMapProps = true`.
- Debit cards with PINs, locking, replacement and card limits, handed out as a `bank_card` inventory item. The item entry and picture are in `install/ox_inventory/`; until the item is added, cards stay bank-only. Set `cards.useItem = false` to keep them bank-only.
- Shared accounts and business accounts with per-person permissions, spending limits and payroll.
- Optional interest, tax, loans, credit score, overdraft and scheduled payments, each off until you turn it on.
- Fraud flags for admins to review. Nothing happens to a player automatically.
- Discord logs per category.
- Admin dashboard with account search, freezing, adjustments, reversals, fraud alerts, the audit log, loan approvals, ATM management and a balance check.
- Exports for other resources: accounts, balances, deposits, withdrawals, transfers, society money, loans, credit scores, payroll and scheduled payments.
