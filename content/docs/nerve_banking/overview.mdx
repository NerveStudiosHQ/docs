Nerve Studios Banking gives every character a real bank. Players open savings and shared accounts at a branch, order debit cards with a PIN, and use ATMs that show the bank right on the machine's screen. Jobs get society accounts, businesses get accounts with employees and payroll, and the city gets a treasury. Every payment is recorded on both sides, so every balance can be checked against its history.

It runs on ESX and QBox. The banking screen and the ATM screen ship ready to use, with no build step.

## Features

- **Six account types:** personal checking, savings, shared, business, society and government. Each account has its own 8-digit account number, with a copy button wherever it is shown.
- **Your framework's bank money is the checking account.** Paychecks, shops and every other script that pays into or charges the bank keep working as before.
- **Deposits, withdrawals and transfers** at bank branches, with a confirmation step that shows who receives the money. Players pick one of their own accounts or a player standing nearby in one click, or type an account number.
- **Limits per transaction and per day** for players, for each person on a shared or business account, and for each card. Optional fees on deposits, withdrawals, transfers and ATM withdrawals.
- **Full history and monthly statements** for every account.
- **Nothing is ever paid twice.** Repeated clicks, lag and reconnects never charge twice, and two payments from one account at the same moment can never spend the same money.
- **A banking screen laid out like a bank app:** total balance, account cards, recent transactions, your debit card, weekly spending and today's limits.
- **A lock screen.** The banking screen opens locked. Players unlock it with the PIN of one of their debit cards, or by holding a button when they have no card.
- **ATMs that show the bank on the machine's own screen.** The camera moves in front of the machine and the player uses it with the mouse and the number keys: insert a card, type the PIN, withdraw, deposit, check a mini statement or change the PIN. Machines that can't show it get a full-screen ATM terminal instead.
- **Debit cards** with hashed PINs, lockout after wrong PINs, locking, replacement, cancelling and card limits, handed out as a `bank_card` inventory item.
- **Shared accounts** with per-person permissions and spending limits.
- **Business accounts** with employees, salaries and payroll, created by your other scripts or from jobs.
- **Society accounts** for your jobs, with access by job grade, and **government accounts** for tax and fines.
- **Optional modules,** all off until you turn them on: interest on savings, tax with brackets and tax-free holidays, loans with approval and automatic repayments, credit scores, overdrafts and fraud flags. Scheduled payments are on by default.
- **An admin dashboard:** totals and 14-day volume, account search, freeze and unfreeze, adjustments and reversals with a reason, fraud alerts, the audit log, loan approvals, ATM management and a balance check.
- **Discord logs** in 18 categories, each with its own webhook if you want.
- **Discord profile pictures** for players who have Discord linked.
- **Your server's look:** your accent colour on both screens, and your logo at the foot of the side menu.
- **Exports** for shops, jobs, robberies, fines, hospitals and phones, and hooks for your own notify, inventory, admin checks and logging.
- **Translatable.** Every player-facing text is in the locale file.

## How it plays

1. A player walks up to a bank counter and opens the bank with ox_target (or a key prompt, if you prefer).
2. The banking screen opens on the lock screen. The player types the PIN of one of their cards, or holds the button if they have none yet.
3. At the counter they deposit and withdraw cash, transfer money, open savings or shared accounts, add people to a shared account and order a debit card.
4. The card lands in their inventory. At any ATM they insert it, type the PIN and take out cash.
5. Bosses and permitted grades manage their job's society account from the same screen. Business accounts show their employees and run payroll.

Cash deposits and withdrawals always happen at a bank counter. Transfers do too, unless you set `transferAnywhere = true` in `shared/config_limits.lua`.

## The lock screen [#the-lock-screen]

The banking screen opens locked, so nobody can use a player's bank while they are away from the keyboard.

- **With a debit card,** the player types the PIN of any of their cards. A wrong PIN counts towards the lockout of their cards, the same as at an ATM. Once the cards are blocked, the bank stays locked until the block ends (`lockMinutes` in `shared/config_cards.lua`).
- **Without a card,** the player presses and holds the button, or holds Space.
- The bank stays unlocked for `ui.unlockMinutes` (10 by default). Players can lock it again from the side menu.

Set `ui.unlock = 'hold'` in `shared/config.lua` to always use the hold button, or `false` for no lock screen.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| ESX Legacy or QBox | Characters, jobs, cash and bank money |
| [ox_lib](https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib/releases) | Interface helpers, callbacks and language |
| [oxmysql](https://github.com/overextended/oxmysql/releases) | Stores accounts, history, cards and loans. MariaDB 10.2+ or MySQL 8.0.16+ |
| [ox_target](https://github.com/overextended/ox_target/releases) | Optional. Interacting with bank counters and ATMs. Without it, players get a key prompt instead |
| [ox_inventory](https://github.com/overextended/ox_inventory/releases) | Optional. For the debit card item. Without it, cards stay in the bank |
| OneSync | Required |
| Server artifact 5848 or newer | Required |

The database tables are created on the first start. There is no SQL to import by hand.

## Performance

- About 0.00 ms in the client resmon while the player is not at a bank or an ATM.
- Only one ATM screen is ever loaded per player, and it is unloaded after a short idle time.
- The console stays silent unless `debug = true`, apart from warnings and errors you need to act on.
