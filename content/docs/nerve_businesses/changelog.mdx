## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16) [#v1-0-0]

#### Added
- The Business Manager: every job on the server with create, rename, copy and delete, ranks with salaries and drag to reorder, a business per job and public features no job owns. Pick a business from the top bar, filter by category and see every feature in one table grouped by type, with editors in a side panel so the list stays in view.
- 23 placeable feature types, picked from a searchable menu grouped by category, with per-job access, live placement, ghost previews, a precision gizmo and copy between businesses.
- Trays, storage, self-emptying trash cans, freezers that stop food going off, personal lockers and branded business boxes.
- Cash registers with commission and tips, shops that pay the business, supply runs and home delivery.
- Crafting stations, the Recipe Manager and the boss's Product Manager.
- Job garages, boss menus, clothing, garages, menu boards, duty points, elevators, peds, props, seats and blips.
- Activity log per business, Discord logs by category, exports and hooks.
- Your accent colour on every screen, with large, clear text that scales from 720p to 4K.
