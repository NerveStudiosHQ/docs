Open the menu with `/blips`. Press **Esc** to close it.

The command name comes from `Command` in `shared/config.lua`. If you set a default key in `Keybind`, players can change it under **Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM**.

## Categories

The side bar on the left rests as a slim strip of icons, so the blip list gets the room. Point at it and it opens over the list with the category names.

| Category | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **My blips** | Your private blips, plus the blips you share with others |
| **Shared with me** | Blips other players shared with you |
| **Groups** | Your blip groups. Open one to see and add its blips. See [Blip groups](/nerve_blipcreator/blip-groups) |
| **Global blips** | Server blips every player sees. Only staff with global access can change them |
| **Requests** | Blip and group invites waiting for your answer |
| **Access** | Full-access admins only. Choose which ACE groups can use the blip creator. See [Global blips and access](/nerve_blipcreator/global-blips-and-access) |

Each list has a search box and a filter. The filter shows **All**, only **Point**, **Radius** or **Area** blips, or only **Hidden** ones.

## Create a blip

1. Go to **My blips** and click **New blip**.
2. Pick a **Type**:
   - **Point** is a single icon.
   - **Radius** is a filled circle.
   - **Area** is a filled rectangle you can resize and rotate.
3. Type a **Name**. This is what shows in the map legend.
4. Click **Change** next to **Icon** and pick one of the map icons. Search by name or ID, or filter by category. Radius and area blips can also show an icon in the middle; turn it off with **Centre icon**.
5. Pick a **Colour**.
6. Set the **Location** (see below).
7. Set the size: **Radius** for circles, or **Width**, **Height** and **Rotation** for areas.
8. Choose where it shows under **Show on**: **Map**, **Minimap** or **Both**.
9. Adjust **Icon size** and **Opacity** if you like. Turn on **Short range** to only show it on the minimap when you are close.
10. Click **Create blip**.

The blip appears on your map straight away.

## Setting the location

You never need to know coordinates. Under **Location** you can use:

- **My position** places it where you are standing.
- **Waypoint** places it on your current waypoint.
- **Pick on map** closes the menu and opens the pause map. Place a waypoint where you want the blip and the menu comes back with that spot filled in. Close the map without placing one to cancel.

You can also type exact **X**, **Y** and **Z** values.

<Callout type="info">

**Pick on map** puts your old waypoint back afterwards, so you don't lose your route.

</Callout>

## Preview on the map

Click **Preview on map** in the editor to see the blip on the pause map before saving. It flashes so it's easy to spot. Close the map to return to the editor.

## Edit or delete

Click a blip you own to edit it. Every blip also has a menu button with these options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set waypoint** | Sets a waypoint to the blip |
| **Hide from my map** / **Show on my map** | Hides or shows the blip for you only |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy |
| **Share** | Opens the share window (your own blips only) |
| **Edit** | Opens the editor |
| **Delete** / **Leave** | Deletes your blip, or leaves a shared one |

## Hide a blip

Open the menu on any blip and click **Hide from my map**. This works on your blips, shared blips, group blips and global blips, and only affects you. Hidden blips stay in the list, dimmed, so you can show them again later. Use the **Hidden** filter to see just those. A hidden blip stays hidden when you share it or make it private again.

## Duplicate a blip

Open the menu on a blip, click **Duplicate** and choose where the copy goes:

- **At my position**
- **At my waypoint**
- **Pick on map**
- **Same spot**

The copy is a new private blip in **My blips**. Staff with global access can turn on **Create as global** to make the copy a global blip instead. Duplicating a shared or global blip is the easy way to keep your own copy of it.

## Limits

Each player can keep up to `Limits.PrivateBlips` private blips (50 by default). The counter next to **New blip** shows how many you have.

If you lower a limit, nothing is deleted. Players keep every blip they already have, and can't add new ones until they are under the limit. See [Configuration](/nerve_blipcreator/configuration#config) for every limit.
