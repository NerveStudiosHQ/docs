---
title: "Blip groups"
description: "Create a group, invite your crew, and everyone in it sees the blips added to the group."
---

A blip group is a small team, like a gang, crew, company or group of friends. Everyone in the group sees every blip added to it, and nobody outside the group does. Members can add their own blips to the group at any time.

| | Default | Option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players in one group, including the owner | 10 | `Groups.MaxMembers` |
| Groups one player can create | 3 | `Groups.MaxOwned` |
| Groups one player can be in, including their own | 5 | `Groups.MaxJoined` |
| Blips in one group | 50 | `Groups.MaxBlips` |
| Longest group name | 24 | `Groups.NameLength` |

<Callout type="info">

Blip groups are made by players and have nothing to do with ACE groups like `group.admin`. Groups are also different from [sharing a single blip](/nerve_blipcreator/sharing): sharing gives people one of your blips, while a group is a space where every member can add blips for the whole group.

</Callout>

## Create a group

1. Open the menu and click **Groups** in the side bar.
2. Click **New group**.
3. Type a name and click **Create group**.

You are the owner. The group opens straight away.

## Invite players

Only the owner can invite people, and the player must be standing near you.

1. Open the group and click **Members**.
2. Under **Invite a player**, type the player's server ID and press **Enter**.
3. The player gets a notification, and the invite shows in their **Requests**.
4. They click **Accept** and join the group.

Invites expire after `Sharing.InviteTimeout` seconds (60 by default). The player must be within `Sharing.NearbyDistance` metres when you invite them. You can't invite someone if the group is full or they are already in the maximum number of groups.

## Add blips to a group

1. Open the group.
2. Click **Add blip**.
3. Fill in the editor the same way as a normal blip (see [Using the menu](/nerve_blipcreator/using-the-menu#create-a-blip)) and click **Create blip**.

Every member sees the new blip on their map straight away. The list shows who added each blip.

## Who can do what

| | Owner | Member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See every group blip | Yes | Yes |
| Add blips | Yes | Yes |
| Edit or delete the blips they added | Yes | Yes |
| Edit or delete anyone's blips | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove members | Yes | No |
| Rename the group | Yes | No |
| Delete the group | Yes | No |
| Leave the group | No, delete it instead | Yes |

Every member can also hide any group blip from their own map, or duplicate it into their private blips, from the blip's menu.

## Manage the group

From the group's menu, or next to **Members** inside it:

- **Rename group** changes the name for everyone.
- **Delete group** removes the group and all of its blips for every member.
- **Leave group** (members only) takes the group's blips off your map. The owner can invite you again later.

To remove someone, open **Members** and click **Remove** next to their name. Their map updates straight away. Blips they added stay in the group.

## Turning groups off

Set `Groups.Enabled = false` in `shared/config.lua`. The **Groups** category disappears from the menu, and existing groups stay saved in the database in case you turn it back on.

Group invites use the nearby invite, so they also stop working when `Sharing.Enabled` or `Sharing.Nearby` is `false`.
