---
title: "Blip groups"
description: "Create a group, invite your crew, and everyone in it sees the blips added to the group."
---

A blip group is a small team, like a gang, crew, company or group of friends. Everyone in the group sees every blip added to it, and nobody outside the group does. Members can add their own blips to the group at any time.

| | Default | Option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players in one group, including the owner | 10 | `Config.Groups.MaxMembers` |
| Groups one player can create | 3 | `Config.Groups.MaxOwned` |
| Groups one player can be in | 5 | `Config.Groups.MaxJoined` |
| Blips in one group | 50 | `Config.Groups.MaxBlips` |

<Callout type="info">

Groups are different from [sharing a single blip](/nerve_blipcreator/sharing). Sharing gives people one of your blips. A group is a shared space where every member can add blips for the whole group.

</Callout>

## Create a group

1. Open the menu and click **Groups** on the left.
2. Click **New group**.
3. Type a name and click **Create group**.

You are the owner. The group opens straight away.

## Invite players

Only the owner can invite people.

1. Open the group and click **Members**.
2. Type the player's server ID and click **Invite**, or click **Invite** next to someone under the nearby list.
3. The player gets a notification, and the invite shows in their **Requests**.
4. They click **Accept** and join the group.

Invites expire after `Config.Sharing.InviteTimeout` seconds (60 by default). You can't invite someone if the group is full or they are already in the maximum number of groups.

## Add blips to a group

1. Open the group.
2. Click **Add blip**.
3. Fill in the editor the same way as a normal blip (see [Using the menu](/nerve_blipcreator/using-the-menu#create-a-blip)) and click **Create blip**.

Every member sees the new blip on their map straight away. The list shows who added each blip.

## Who can do what

| | Owner | Member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See every group blip | Yes | Yes |
| Add blips | Yes | Yes |
| Edit or delete their own blips | Yes | Yes |
| Edit or delete anyone's blips | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove members | Yes | No |
| Rename the group | Yes | No |
| Delete the group | Yes | No |
| Leave the group | No, delete it instead | Yes |

Every member can also hide any group blip from their own map, or duplicate it into their private blips, from the **⋯** menu.

## Manage the group

From the **⋯** menu on a group, or next to **Members** inside it:

- **Rename group** changes the name for everyone.
- **Delete group** removes the group and all of its blips for every member.
- **Leave group** (members only) takes the group's blips off your map. The owner can invite you again later.

To remove someone, open **Members** and click **Remove** next to their name. Their map updates straight away. Blips they added stay in the group.

## Security

The server checks every group action, so players can't get into groups they weren't invited to or change blips they don't own.

- **Membership is checked on every action** on the server: viewing, adding, editing and deleting group blips.
- **Invites can't be faked.** A player can only join with an invite the server sent to them. Invites can't be reused, shared with someone else, or used after they expire.
- **Owner-only actions are checked.** Inviting, removing members, renaming and deleting all verify that you own the group.
- **Edit rights are checked.** Editing or deleting a blip checks that you added it or own the group.
- **Limits are enforced on the server**, including when several players act at the same time.
- **Removal is instant.** A player who leaves or is removed loses the group's blips straight away.
- **Clean input.** Group and blip names are cleaned of colour codes and formatting, and every blip field is validated.
- **Rate limits.** Every action is rate limited.

## Turning groups off

Set `Config.Groups.Enabled = false` in `config.lua`. The **Groups** category disappears from the menu, and existing groups stay saved in the database in case you turn it back on.
