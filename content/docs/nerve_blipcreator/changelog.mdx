## 1.0.0

First release.

- Private blips saved on each player's PC
- Shared blips with nearby invites and share codes
- Blip groups where every member sees and adds group blips
- Admin-only global blips
- Access tab for staff groups
- Point, radius and area blips with 956 icons and 41 colours
- Map picker, waypoint and current position placement, with a preview on the map
- Hide and one-click duplicate for any blip
- Support for 11 notification systems plus custom notifications
- Nine languages
- Exports for global blips
