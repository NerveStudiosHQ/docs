Nerve Studios Blip Creator is an in-game blip editor for FiveM. Players open one menu to create, edit and share blips on the pause map and minimap. Admins use the same menu to add global blips every player sees, and to decide which staff groups may do that.

It runs on ESX and QBox, and needs no framework at all, so it also works on a standalone server. The menu ships ready to use, with no build step.

## Kinds of blips

| Kind | Who sees it | Who can make it | Where it is saved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Private** | Only the player who made it | Every player | On the player's own PC |
| **Shared** | The owner and everyone who joined | Every player | On the server |
| **Group** | Everyone in the blip group | Group members | On the server |
| **Global** | Every player on the server | Admins with global access | On the server |

A new blip starts private. When a player shares it, it is saved on the server and everyone who joins sees the owner's changes live. Global blips are separate, and only staff with global access can add them.

## Features

- **Three blip types.** A point with an icon, a radius circle, or an area rectangle with width, height and rotation.
- **956 map icons** in a searchable picker with categories, and 41 GTA colours.
- **Easy placement.** Use your position, your waypoint, or pick a spot on the pause map. You can also type coordinates.
- **Preview on the map** before you save.
- **Display options.** Show on the map, the minimap or both, with icon size, opacity and short range.
- **Duplicate** a blip to your position, your waypoint, a spot on the map or the same spot.
- **Hide any blip** from your own map, including shared and global blips, and set a waypoint to any blip.
- **Share with players near you** or with an **invite code**. Everyone who joins sees your changes live.
- **Blip groups.** Create a group, invite players, and everyone sees the blips any member adds to it.
- **Global blips** placed by admins for every player on the server.
- **Access panel** where admins choose which ACE groups may create personal or global blips, without editing `server.cfg`.
- **Map legend categories** for private, shared, group and global blips.
- **Limits for everything:** blips per player, members per shared blip, group sizes, name lengths, sizes and icons.
- **Your server's look.** Set the menu's highlight colour and logo from the config.
- **Works with your notifications** through one hook, with ready examples for the common notification resources.
- **Nine languages:** English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese and Turkish.
- **Server exports** to create, edit and remove global blips from your own scripts.

## Performance

- About 0.00 ms in the client resmon while idle. Blips are drawn once and only redrawn when they change.
- Private blips never touch the server or the database.
- Changes are saved to the database in small batches, so busy editing doesn't load the server.
- The console stays silent unless `Debug = true`, apart from warnings and errors you need to know about.

## Security

The server checks every request, so players can't create blips they aren't allowed to, change other players' blips or spam the server.

- Every field is validated against your limits: coordinates, sizes, icons, colours and names.
- Blip and group names are cleaned of colour codes and formatting.
- Permissions are checked with ACE on every request.
- The distance for nearby invites is measured on the server.
- Every action is rate limited, and too many wrong share codes lock a player out for a while.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| [ox_lib](https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib/releases) | Language and notifications |
| [oxmysql](https://github.com/overextended/oxmysql/releases) | Saves shared, group and global blips |
| OneSync | Required. On by default on current servers |
| Framework | None needed. Works on ESX, QBox or a standalone server |

Game build **3258** or newer shows every icon. Older builds work too: lower `Sprites.Max` so players only pick icons your build can draw.
