---
title: "Sharing"
description: "Share blips with players near you or with a share code, so your crew or gang all see the same blip."
---

Any player with access to the menu can share their own blips. There are two ways to add people, and you can use both on the same blip.

| | Nearby invite | Share code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | One or two people next to you | A whole crew, gang or business |
| How they join | Accept the invite in **Requests** | Enter the code under **Join with a share code** |
| Needs them close by | Yes | No |

Only the people who joined see a shared blip. When the owner edits it, every member's map updates straight away.

## Share with someone near you

1. Stand next to the player.
2. In **My blips**, open the menu on your blip and click **Share**.
3. Under **Players near you**, click **Invite** next to their name. Click **Refresh** if they just walked up.
4. They get a notification and the invite shows in their **Requests** category.
5. They click **Accept**, and the blip appears on their map.

Invites expire after `Sharing.InviteTimeout` seconds (60 by default). You are told when someone accepts or declines.

<Callout type="info">

Only players within `Sharing.NearbyDistance` metres (15 by default) appear in the list. The server checks the distance, so it can't be faked.

</Callout>

## Share with a code

1. Open the menu on your blip and click **Share**.
2. Click **Create share code**. You get an 8-character code like `K7P2-QX9M`.
3. Click **Copy** and send it to your crew.
4. They open the menu, click **Join with a share code**, paste the code and press **Enter**.

Everyone who enters the code joins the blip, and only they see it. This makes it ideal for gang turf, crew meet-ups and business locations. Codes ignore spaces, dashes and upper or lower case.

To control who can still join:

- **New code** makes a fresh code. The old one stops working, and people who already joined stay in.
- **Turn off code** stops anyone new from joining with a code.

<Callout type="info">

A player who enters too many wrong codes is locked out of codes for a while. This stops anyone from guessing codes.

</Callout>

## Manage members

The share window lists everyone who joined.

- Click **Remove** to take someone out. The blip disappears from their map straight away.
- **Stop sharing** removes every member and makes the blip private again, saved on your PC.
- Deleting a shared blip removes it for everyone.

## As a member

Shared blips show in **Shared with me** with the owner's name. You can:

- **Hide** it from your map.
- **Duplicate** it to keep your own private copy.
- **Leave** it from the blip's menu. You can join again with a new invite or the code.

You can't edit someone else's blip. Only the owner can change it.

## Private and shared

A new blip is private and lives only on your PC. The first time you invite someone or create a code, the blip is saved on the server so the people who join can see it. It still shows in **My blips**, with a **Shared** label and the member count.

## Limits

| Option | Default | What it limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Limits.SharedBlips` | 20 | How many blips one player can share |
| `Limits.JoinedBlips` | 50 | How many shared blips one player can join |
| `Limits.MembersPerBlip` | 50 | How many people can join one shared blip |
| `Sharing.MaxInvites` | 5 | Open invites one player can have waiting at once |

To turn sharing off completely, set `Sharing.Enabled = false` in `shared/config.lua`. This also turns off group invites. To keep only one of the two methods, set `Sharing.Nearby` or `Sharing.Codes` to `false`.
